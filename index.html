Why 4:30

Chasing the golden hour

We meet with time to scout. Then the sun drops behind Jaipur, the sandstone turns gold, and after sunset the museum lights up against a deep blue sky. These times are worked out for Albert Hall on 10 October.

Golden hourWarm, low light on the facade. Shoot the domes against the sky and let the arches throw long shadows.
SunsetTurn around. Silhouettes of the domes, the lamps and the pigeons against an orange sky.
Blue hourThe museum lights come on while the sky is still blue. Hold steady, or find something to lean on.

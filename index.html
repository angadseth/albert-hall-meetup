The place

Rajasthan's oldest museum

Albert Hall stands in Ram Niwas Garden, just outside the walls of the old city. It was designed by Samuel Swinton Jacob in the Indo-Saracenic style, mixing Rajput domes and arches with a Victorian plan. Every corner of it is a frame.

1876

Foundation stone laid during the Prince of Wales's visit to Jaipur.

1887

Opens as a public museum.

Indo-Saracenic

Domes, chhatris, jharokhas and arches in carved stone.

After dark

Lit up in the evenings, the building's best-known look.
